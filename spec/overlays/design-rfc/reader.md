# Annex B §B.4.1 — `design-rfc` reader overlay

**ITWS version:** 0.6.0-draft · **Status:** normative

This overlay adds one navigation convention to the §B.1 base reader.

The reader recognizes a proposal organized around context, requirements, a proposed design, alternatives, risks, rollout, and unresolved questions.

Section B.4 states the convention-only boundary that governs this overlay. The convention lets the writer use the corresponding section functions without teaching the genre. The convention does not permit unexplained domain terms inside those sections.
