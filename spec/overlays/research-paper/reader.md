# Annex B §B.4.7 — `research-paper` reader overlay

**ITWS version:** 0.8.0-draft · **Status:** normative

This overlay adds navigation conventions to the §B.1 base reader.

The reader recognizes navigation derived from introduction, methods, results, and discussion (IMRaD). The reader treats citations as source pointers and distinguishes results from discussion.

This overlay expressly assumes no machine-learning knowledge. Every machine-learning term, method, benchmark, dataset, metric, convention, or symbol absent from §B.1 and §B.2 requires admission.

Section B.4 states the convention-only boundary that governs this overlay. The conventions let the writer use the corresponding section functions without teaching the genre. The conventions do not permit unexplained domain terms inside those sections.
